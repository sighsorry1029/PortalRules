PortalRules retains the embedded-English, selected-language overlay, external
translation discovery, and Valheim localization lifecycle concepts. The
adaptation uses PortalRules-owned Harmony patches; performs deterministic,
multi-file merging; confines malformed files and I/O failures; supports YAML,
YML, and JSON overrides; and omits placeholders, AddText, weak-reference state,
JetBrains annotations, and private Localization field access.
